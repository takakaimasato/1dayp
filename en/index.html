1Dayp

A simple photo diary for one photo a day, a little note, the weather and your mood.

Contact 1dayp support

For help using the app, reporting a problem or asking about privacy, please use the contact below.

Operator and contact: 1dayp Support
Email: nnpress.mikamo@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, 1dayp version and the steps that led to it. Do not send passwords or verification codes. Before attaching a screenshot, check that it does not reveal personal information or private diary entries.

If you cannot take a photo

Open Settings on your iPhone, then Apps → 1dayp, and check camera access. The path and options may vary with your iOS version.

If you cannot save

Check the available storage on your device. If you have an unsaved photo, do not delete the app. If you see “Couldn't save”, try “Retry”.

About your data

Your photo diary is stored on your device. Deleting the app or a device failure may cause you to lose your entries. Use a device backup to help protect important records. The current release provides Camera, Memories and MONTHLY. It does not offer Fever Time, photo sharing or in-app purchases.

Language and note limits

In the bilingual version, open “Language” and choose “日本語” (Japanese), “English” or “Use device settings”. Notes have an input limit of 30 characters in Japanese mode and 60 characters in English mode.

Switching languages never automatically shortens a saved note. If you edit a longer English-mode note while using Japanese mode, changes over 30 characters cannot be saved, but the text stays on screen. Switch back to English mode if you need to edit the longer note.

Camera and photo access

Take photos with the camera inside the app. The current regular diary does not import photos from your photo library or automatically add captured photos to the Photos app. Saving a diary entry does not require access to your entire photo library.

Connections outside the app

Regular diary photos, notes, weather and moods are saved and displayed on your device. They are not sent to the operator's server or Firebase. Weather and mood are chosen manually, and location is not accessed. Sending a support email or visiting this website involves connections to external services. PetaCal

PetaCal is a simple calendar app for managing plans by choosing a stamp and tapping a date.

Main features

  • Create custom stamps
  • Apply a stamp to multiple dates
  • Manage shifts
  • Set a start time for each plan
  • Add a short note to a date
  • Change the theme color
  • Choose sound effects
  • Use haptic feedback

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a stamp?
Tap the “+” at the top right, then choose an emoji, name, color and other settings.
Can I use the same stamp on several dates?
Yes. Once you select a stamp, you can apply it to multiple dates in succession.
How do I view the details for a date?
With no stamp selected, tap a date to see its plans, times and notes.
Where is my data stored?
PetaCal's plans, stamps and settings are generally stored on your device.
Do I need an account?
No. You can use PetaCal without registering an account.
Can I share a calendar?
The current version does not offer calendar sharing.

Read the PetaCal Privacy Policy for information about how its data is handled.
