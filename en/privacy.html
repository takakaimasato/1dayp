1DAYP · CURRENT FEATURES

Diary data and permissions

Bilingual information and specification clarification: November 9, 2026. This summary describes the current regular diary and the bilingual version. The existing Japanese policy and information for users of earlier test versions are retained below and translated here.

  • Photos, capture dates, notes, manually selected weather and moods, drafts, monthly magazine edits and language preferences are handled on your device. Regular diary entries are not sent to the operator's server or Firebase.
  • The app asks for camera permission to take photos. The current regular diary does not read your photo library or automatically add photos to the Photos app. It does not use the microphone or location.
  • Fever Time, photo sharing, QR invitations, cloud sign-in and in-app purchases are not currently offered. The cloud descriptions in sections 3 and 5 concern earlier test versions.
  • If you email support, information is handled through Gmail. When you visit this website, information is handled through GitHub Pages. These are separate from your on-device diary; see sections 4 and 6.

Device and iCloud backups are separate mechanisms controlled by your operating system and device settings. They are not an app-operated cloud sync or backup service. If you have important entries, check your backup arrangements without deleting the app.

About the local diary release

The release described in the existing policy as “Version 1.0 (Build 9 and later)” is an on-device photo diary providing Camera, Memories and MONTHLY. It does not provide Fever Time, shared photos, QR invitations, cloud account creation or sign-in, or in-app purchases.

Its diary features do not send photos, notes, selected weather or moods to the operator or Firebase. They do not use advertising or tracking analytics. Support emails and this website are handled separately, as described in sections 4 and 6.

The cloud descriptions in sections 3 and 5 apply to information provided through earlier test versions with Fever features. Earlier test data is not automatically migrated to or published through the new public release, and on-device photos are not automatically uploaded. The new public release has no cloud account controls. If you used an earlier test version and need help accessing or deleting cloud information, contact the support address in section 9.

1. Photo diary information

Photos you take, their capture dates, short notes, selected weather and moods, and monthly magazine edits are handled on your device to save, display and revisit your entries. The regular photo diary's save process does not upload these records to the operator's server.

You select the weather manually. Recording the weather does not access your current location.

2. Device permissions

The current regular diary asks for camera permission to take photos. The photo export mentioned below belongs to the earlier Fever features, which are currently disabled. That export uses permission to add photos, not permission to read the entire photo library.

The camera is used to take photos. When an image is exported to the Photos app, the necessary permission is requested. You can change permissions in your iPhone settings. If you do not grant a permission, the corresponding feature cannot be used.

3. Cloud sharing, identity verification and purchases in earlier test versions

Regular photo diaries and photos from Fever Time used alone are handled on the device. In earlier test versions, choosing to invite friends during Fever Time or joining through an invitation QR code uses Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication to verify identity for cloud features. There is no separate app-specific email and password registration.

Cloud features handle an Apple-authentication-based identifier, Firebase user ID, authentication status, participation records and the display name you set. These are used for identity verification, access control, displaying participants and preventing misuse. The information obtained through Sign in with Apple also depends on Apple's settings and earlier consent.

Photos taken for sharing after sharing begins, photo IDs, authors, storage locations, sizes and timestamps, and Fever start and end times, participant counts and photo counts are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage for Firebase. They are shown to participants in the same invitation-only Fever. There is no public feed or user search. Photos saved on your device before an invitation and earlier diary photos are not automatically uploaded.

Purchases use Apple StoreKit and in-app purchases. For purchase verification, restoration and fraud prevention, the features handle product IDs, Apple transaction identifiers, purchase status, an app-specific purchase association token and user ID. Cloud Functions queries Apple's servers to verify transactions. The operator does not obtain credit card numbers or payment details. Participants are not required to purchase Fever Time.

Reports store identifiers for the reported photo, reporter and reported user, the chosen reason and handling status. Hiding and blocking store the relevant identifiers and status. Reports are not made public to regular participants. The operator may review reports and the relevant shared photos when needed for safety and support, and may remove problematic photos. When blocking someone, a host removes the other participant, or a participant leaves the session themselves. The server restricts rejoining.

Firebase and Google Cloud handle technical logs, such as processing times, requests and errors, to provide the service, investigate problems and maintain security. The features do not use advertising, tracking analytics, Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Photos are not sent to an additional AI image moderation service. For details about external services, see Firebase Privacy and Security, the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Support information

Contact information, message text and optional attachments you provide when requesting support are used to reply, investigate the issue and provide assistance. You do not need to send diary photos or other personal information that is not needed to investigate your question.

Support emails are received, stored and answered through Google's Gmail. Your email address, message and attachments are therefore handled through Google's services as part of sending, receiving and storing email. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's handling of information.

Support information is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and check related issues. Information is deleted as it is no longer needed. Support inquiries are not used for advertising.

5. Storage, deletion and backups

Deleting the app removes its on-device photo diary records. In iOS, “Offload App” is different from deleting the app and keeps its documents and data. Copies saved to the Photos app and device, iCloud or other backups are managed separately. Backup handling depends on device settings and the service provider's terms.

The operator cannot remotely view, delete or restore records stored on your device.

The following paragraphs on shared albums and cloud accounts concern earlier test versions. The current regular diary does not provide these cloud features.

Shared albums can be viewed for 30 days after Fever ends. After that period, scheduled processing gradually deletes shared photos and related session information. Records required for blocking, identity checks and purchase verification are retained separately from the album.

In the cloud features, an account is deleted through the “Delete Fever cloud account” control on the Fever screen after confirmation and Apple reauthentication. This revokes authentication, deletes the Firebase user, and deletes the user's shared photos and participation records. If a host deletes their account, their entire shared Fever, including other participants' photos, becomes subject to deletion and other participants lose access. Some purchase records remain in an invalidated state to prevent fraudulent reuse. This is separate from on-device diary entries and copies exported to the Photos app.

Deleting the app from a device does not by itself delete a cloud account. The operator cannot remotely delete copies that participants have saved to their Photos app. Deleted cloud objects may remain for up to seven days under service recovery settings. Technical logs and support information are kept for the periods needed for their purposes and the relevant service settings.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The operator does not use their own advertising, analytics scripts or external fonts on it. GitHub Pages records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of information.

7. Information management and sharing with third parties

Support information received by the operator is handled only as needed for assistance and managed appropriately to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. It is not provided to third parties except as necessary for the external services described in this policy, with the individual's consent, or as required by law.

Support uses Gmail as described in section 4, and the website uses GitHub Pages as described in section 6. When using external services or service providers, information is limited to what is needed for the purpose. The operator reviews the provider's terms and security practices and seeks to protect information in line with this policy. Any new provider entrusted with handling support information will also be required to provide appropriate protection.

8. Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if features or information handling change. Significant changes will also be announced through the app or other appropriate means when needed.

PetaCal

The following describes information handling in PetaCal's current implementation. The Firebase descriptions for 1Dayp do not apply to PetaCal.

Accounts

PetaCal does not require account registration and has no sign-in feature.

Calendar data and settings

Stamps, plans, start times and short notes you create for dates are stored on your device using SwiftData. Theme, sound effect and haptic feedback preferences are stored in on-device UserDefaults. They are used to display and edit plans and configure the app.

External transmission

PetaCal does not currently use Firebase, an operator-run server, advertising SDKs, usage analytics SDKs or crash reporting SDKs. It has no process for sending your plans, stamps, times, notes or settings to an external server. SwiftData CloudKit synchronization is not used.

If you send a support email or visit this website, the handling of that information is separate from on-device app storage. See the shared terms below.

Advertising and sharing with third parties

PetaCal does not currently display advertising. The operator does not collect on-device data such as your plans and notes from PetaCal and does not provide that data to third parties. Information you provide in support requests is handled under the shared terms.

Storage, deletion and backups

You can delete stamps and notes in the app. Deleting a stamp also deletes the plans and start times on dates that use that stamp. Notes attached to dates are stored independently.

Deleting the app removes its on-device data and settings. iOS “Offload App” keeps data and differs from deleting the app. Device and iCloud or other backups are managed separately according to device settings and the service provider's terms. The operator cannot remotely view, delete or restore on-device data.

Terms shared by both apps and this website

Support requests for 1Dayp and PetaCal are accepted at nnpress.mikamo@gmail.com, the existing 1dayp Support contact.

Sections 4 (Support information), 6 (This website), 7 (Information management and sharing with third parties), 8 (Changes to this policy) and 9 (Contact) under 1Dayp above also apply to PetaCal support and this website. Gmail is used for support emails, and GitHub Pages is used to host this website; information is handled through those respective services.

You do not need to attach diary photos, plans, notes or other personal information that is not needed for your request. For questions about information handling, use the shared support contact.
